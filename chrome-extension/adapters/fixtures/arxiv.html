Title:Ledger-scale weather reconstruction from lighthouse logbooks

Authors:Ada Renn, Kit Moreau, Ifeoma Okonkwo
Abstract:Keepers of manned lighthouses recorded visibility, wind and sea state at fixed hours for over a century. We digitise 41 years of one such ledger and show that the resulting series closes a documented gap in the coastal reanalysis record, with a mean bias of 0.3 m/s against the nearest instrumented station.
Subjects: Atmospheric and Oceanic Physics (physics.ao-ph); Digital Libraries (cs.DL)
Cite as: arXiv:2403.00219 [physics.ao-ph]
