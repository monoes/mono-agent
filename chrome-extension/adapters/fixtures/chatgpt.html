Why did the Dunmore ledger survive when the rest of the station's records did not?

Survival bias, mostly. The ledger was in a drawer that had been painted shut during a 1974 refit, so it was not in the pile that went to the tip.

You can test that claim against the decommissioning dates:

python
survivors = [s for s in stations if s.decommissioned < 1975]
print(len(survivors))
  • Stations closed before 1975 kept their logbooks on site.
  • Later closures were centralised, and centralised means skipped.
